1. What courses do you offer?
EduReach College offers B.Tech programs in CSE, ECE, AI and Data Science, Mechanical, Civil, and IT; M.Tech programs in Computer Science, VLSI Design, and Structural Engineering; and an MBA with Finance, Marketing, HR, and IT specializations.

2. What is the fee for Civil Engineering?
Civil Engineering is a B.Tech program. The listed 2024–2025 annual fee is ₹1,70,000 for day scholars and ₹2,50,000 for hostellers. Confirm the latest fee with the admissions office.

3. What is the highest placement package?
The supplied 2023–2024 placement information lists the highest package as ₹42 LPA, offered by Google.

4. What is the eligibility for B.Tech admission?
Students need 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM), with at least 60% marks. JEE Main holders receive preference, according to the supplied information.

5. What facilities are available on campus?
Facilities include smart classrooms, advanced laboratories, a central library, campus Wi-Fi, boys' and girls' hostels, a gym, sports grounds, a medical center, an ATM, and a stationery shop.
